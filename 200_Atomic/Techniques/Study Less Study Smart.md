# Study Less, Study Smart

## Core Idea

12 evidence-based learning principles that maximize retention and understanding while minimizing time and effort. Focus on active learning over passive consumption.

## Why It Matters

Most students study inefficiently—rereading, highlighting, cramming. These principles are based on cognitive science research and dramatically improve learning ROI.

## Key Points

### The 12 Principles

1. **Take breaks** - Don't study too long. 5-min breaks after 20-30 min if not enjoying
2. **Reward system** - Positive reinforcement after study sessions
3. **Dedicated study area** - Behavior reinforcement. Separate space = study mode
4. **Active learning > Rote memorization** - Understand before memorizing. Explain in your own words
5. **Study in groups** - Collaborative learning catches blind spots
6. **Highlighting is overrated** - Recognition ≠ Recollection
7. **Don't highlight blindly** - Only mark after understanding
8. **Sleep is critical** - 7-8 hours for memory consolidation (REM sleep)
9. **Always take notes** - Reviewing notes > rereading source
10. **Active recitation** - Teach others what you learned (Feynman approach)
11. **SQ3R method** - Survey, Question, Read, Recite, Review (20% reading, 80% reciting)
12. **Use mnemonics** - For memorizing facts (acronyms, sayings, interactive images)

### Book Reading Strategy (SQ3R Enhanced)
- Feel free to jump chapters (it's not a novel)
- For each chapter: Review images, main points, questions
- Write questions in margins
- Read bold words, titles, names, marked text
- Read first and last sentence of each paragraph
- Read whole book
- Try to answer questions
- Reread and answer previously-skipped questions
- Reread with marker and pencil
- Answer questions, create your own
- Write essay on main topics (explain to a child)

## Examples

### Example 1: Learning Angular
**Wrong approach:**
- Read documentation passively
- Highlight syntax
- Hope it sticks

**Study Smart approach:**
1. Dedicated coding desk (Principle 3)
2. 25-min focused intervals (Principle 1)
3. Build small app (Principle 4 - active learning)
4. Explain to colleague (Principle 10 - teach)
5. Sleep 8 hours (Principle 8)
6. Review notes next day (Principle 9)

### Example 2: Preparing for Interview
- Study group for mock interviews (Principle 5)
- Create acronym for system design steps (Principle 12)
- Practice explaining concepts aloud (Principle 10)
- Take notes after each practice (Principle 9)

## How to Apply

**Immediate changes:**
1. **Set up dedicated study space** - Even if just a specific chair/desk
2. **Stop highlighting while reading** - Only after you understand
3. **End study with active recall** - Explain to imaginary person

**Weekly optimization:**
1. Find study partner/group
2. Review notes within 24 hours of creation
3. Teach one thing you learned to someone

**System-level:**
1. Protect 8 hours sleep (non-negotiable)
2. Build reward system (coffee, walk, game after session)
3. Practice SQ3R on next book

**Implementation checklist:**
- [ ] Dedicated study area set up?
- [ ] Study timer (25 min intervals)?
- [ ] Note-taking system in place?
- [ ] Sleep tracking (7-8 hours)?
- [ ] Teaching opportunities identified?
