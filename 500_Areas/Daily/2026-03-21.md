- Here are the specific **tips and resources** shared in the video:

- **Understand How Code Actually Runs:** Don't treat code as a black box. Learn about **memory allocation**, the **call stack**, and how **garbage collection** affects performance under load.
    
    - **Resource:** The free course **Nand to Tetris** for understanding computing from logic gates up.
- **Go Deep on Systems Design:** Look past interview prep and study real-world engineering decisions.
    
    - **Resource:** Read case studies like **Discord moving from MongoDB to Cassandra**.
    - **Resource:** Read the **Dynamo paper**.
    - **Resource:** Read how **Slack rearchitected message fan-out**.
    - **Resource:** The **Architecture of Open Source Applications** website.
- **Learn a Protocol at the Wire Level:** Pick one (e.g., HTTP, TCP, websockets) and study what is actually on the wire.
    
    - **Resource:** Use **Wireshark** to watch real requests leave your machine.
- **Get Good at Reading Code:** Learn to build a mental model quickly for unfamiliar or legacy codebases.
    
    - **Resource:** Study the code quality of open-source projects like **Linear**, **Excaladraw**, and **Zed**.
- **Build Something That Stays Running:** Take responsibility for a system with uptime requirements to learn about logging, error handling, and degradation.
    
    - **Tip:** Put yourself on call for your own project to understand the realities of breaking software.
