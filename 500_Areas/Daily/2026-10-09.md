- ## Core Domains & Specializations / A career as a researcher

	- **Academic & Basic Research:** Focused on fundamental questions in universities or dedicated institutes. Standard trajectory involves completing a PhD, post-doctoral fellowships, and securing tenure-track faculty or principal investigator (PI) positions funded by grants.
	    
	- **Industry R&D (Corporate):** Applied research aimed at product innovation, optimization, or market execution. Common in pharmaceuticals, tech, biotechnology, hardware, materials science, and energy. Highly collaborative with faster execution cycles than academia.
	    
	- **Data & Applied AI Research:** Leverages machine learning, statistical modeling, and quantitative analysis to build algorithms, analyze systems, or forecast outcomes in tech, finance, and specialized engineering fields.
	    
	- **Qualitative & Social Research:** Focuses on human behavior, policy, UX/market dynamics, or social systems. Employs user interviews, ethnography, surveys, and behavioral data—critical in product design, public policy, and management consulting.
	    
	- **Clinical & Translational Research:** Bridges laboratory discoveries and real-world medical treatments. Involves clinical trials, epidemiology, and health outcomes research across hospitals, pharma, and contract research organizations (CROs).
